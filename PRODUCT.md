# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Wedding guests receiving a digital invitation for John V Alukkal and Aswani Kishore’s Christian–Hindu wedding. They open the link on a phone to learn the essentials and get to the venue.

## Product Purpose

A single-page digital wedding invitation card that presents only essential wedding information: the couple’s names, wedding date and time, a live countdown to the ceremony, and the venue with a shareable location link.

Success means a guest can see who is marrying, when, how soon, and where to go—without scrolling through long copy or secondary details.

## Positioning

A respectful Christian–Hindu union invitation that keeps both traditions present without letting either dominate, delivered as a minimal modern digital card rather than a multi-section wedding microsite.

## Operating Context

Opened primarily on mobile (shared via WhatsApp / messaging). Venue is Flora Charishma Residency; location is shared via Google Maps short link. Guests may tap Share Location to open maps and navigate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show couple names: John V Alukkal & Aswani Kishore
- Show wedding date: October 12, 2026
- Show ceremony time: 11:30 am
- Live countdown to the wedding datetime
- Venue name: Flora Charishma Residency
- Share Location action linking to https://share.google/GFI1miwu2bHCMtHFK
- Do not include: long invitation messages, quotes or Bible verses, RSVP, dress code, family names, reception details, gift information, social media, or decorative filler text
- Stack: existing Next.js (App Router) + React + Tailwind project

## Brand Commitments

- Christian + Hindu cultural harmony, neither religion visually dominant
- Minimal, romantic, premium, elegant, respectful
- Soft warm tones; subtle floral decoration; elegant typography
- Optimized for mobile screens
- Names are the hero; countdown and location are clear but secondary

## Evidence on Hand

- Couple names, date, time, venue name, and Google Maps share URL as provided by the couple
- No photos, logos, or family copy provided; do not invent testimonials or unused ceremony details

## Product Principles

1. Essentials only — every element earns its place on the card
2. Names first — the couple is the primary signal; utilities never overpower them
3. Balanced union — Christian and Hindu cues stay subtle, equal, and respectful
4. Mobile-native — one clear composition that works in a messaging preview and a phone viewport
5. Actionable location — guests can open or share the venue without hunting
